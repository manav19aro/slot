PORTABLE ACHIEVEMENTS

This folder contains downloaded achievement sets, badge images, local progress,
and your Local / Sync Enabled preference. It contains no login credentials.

To move achievements to another device or fresh installation:
1. Shut down the handheld before removing the SD card.
2. Back up this entire Achievements folder.
3. Copy it to the root of the new SD card and use the current Slot build.
4. Add the same ROMs. Filenames and folder locations can be different.

Progress is matched by exact ROM contents. A different region or revision may
require a different achievement set. Back up existing progress on the receiving
card before replacing files.

Settings > Achievements > Achievements turns the feature on or off. Turning it
off preserves downloaded sets, progress, and the selected version.

Local mode needs no account or network after the sets have been downloaded.
Sync Enabled adds explicit buttons to upload local softcore unlocks and import
account progress. Existing local awards are assigned to the signed-in account
on their first explicit Sync. Upload receipts stay bound to that account in
Uploads/. Copy this folder with the rest of Achievements to preserve retries.
Imported awards are never uploaded to another account. There is no background
synchronization. Failed uploads are retained for the next manual Sync.
